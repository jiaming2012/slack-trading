Problem: the fill engine is welded into Playground (simulateTick mutates p.account.PendingOrders directly) and orders take three divergent paths — there is no Broker seam in Simulation at all, and Reconciliation fires down two paths at once.

Solution: extract the fill engine into a Simulated Broker adapter behind the existing IBroker interface; PlaceOrder delegates to the seam in every mode; Reconciliation moves behind the seam.

  • One interface, all three modes
  • Locality: fills concentrate in one adapter
  • Netting exercised in Simulation (ADR-0001)
  • ~12-15 mode branches disappear
  • Tests mock IBroker, not Playground
Implements ADR-0001 — this is the keystone refactor the ADR already commits to.

Make Tick fully absorb time — finish the mode-blind client

Problem: the blocking contract half-exists — the client sleeps in live mode, but a strategy still polls wall-clock itself (mean_reversion_v2), the engine gates OTel spans on is_live, and place_order behaves differently per mode. Nine mode branches across engine and client.

Solution: harden tick() to fully own waiting in every mode; move spans and log-level into platform setup; delete every is_live branch in strategy and engine code.

  • Strategies never sleep or read clocks
  • Same trace/spans in all modes
  • Sim-passing strategy runs unmodified in Margin
  • Fake playground can drive all strategies

Top recommendation

Route every order through the Broker seam

It is the keystone: the Simulated Broker adapter it extracts is what makes the mode-blind client (candidate 3) fully honest, collapses most environment branching, moves Reconciliation behind the seam where ADR-0001 already says it lives, and turns fill-engine testing from "construct a 13-param Playground" into "mock one interface." Candidates 5 and 6 are cheap on-ramps to it; candidate 2 can proceed independently.
